## 4b. `gated_cross_venue`: when a pair is admissible, and why zeros are expected

**A pair is admissible only if all eight stages pass** (`settlement/gate.py`, stage order fixed, every failure recorded in `reasons[]`):

| # | Stage | Passes when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `match` | curated pair, or heuristic Jaccard ≥ 0.6 after the matcher's period / threshold / direction vetoes |
| 2 | `clauses` | both rule texts are readable (cite a source URL) **and** state the same fallback, tie-break and revision treatment; silence on one side is a mismatch |
| 3 | `fingerprint` | explicit structured fingerprints on **both** markets that are `EQUIVALENT` or a safe `COMPLEMENT` (`>=`/`<`, `>`/`<=`) |
| 4 | `polarity` | `EQUIVALENT` with `same_polarity`, or `COMPLEMENT` with inverse polarity |
| 5 | `fed_bucket` | no FOMC bucket on either side, or an `EXACT` bucket match (explicit metadata or derived from ticker/question) |
| 6 | `interval` | no explicit bucket on either side, or identical intervals (complementary half-lines for inverse polarity) |
| 7 | `hosts` | both resolution sources present, `official` tier, same tier, same registrable domain, neither venue's own site |
| 8 | `expiry` | close times unknown on both sides, or known on both and within 72 h |

Then the pair is *priced*, not admitted: `strategies/paper_edge.py` must find a positive **net** edge (dear bid − cheap ask − both venues' fees, walked through depth) at ≥ 1 contract. Admitted-but-flat pairs are counted as `priced_but_no_edge`, not as trades.

**Why zeros are the expected measurement**

| Observation | Why it is correct |
| --- | --- |
| Network runs report `fingerprint_indeterminate` on every pair | Venues publish prose, not structured resolution semantics. Fingerprints must be authored by an operator from the published rules and attached to both markets; the code refuses to infer them. Until that happens, *no* live pair can be admitted, by design. |
| Live Fed pairs are `clause_refuse_mismatch` | Polymarket's Fed-decision text states an explicit rounding rule ("rounded up to the nearest 25 bps"); Kalshi's KXFEDDECISION rules do not. Under a 12.5 bp move the two contracts can settle differently, so the pair is not fungible (arXiv 2601.01706's semantic non-fungibility). |
| `gate_admitted > 0` but `traded == 0` | Settlement-equivalent books rarely cross by more than both fee schedules; `kalshi_fee` rounds up to the cent per fill, so small clips pay proportionally more. |
| Admitted trades show negative ledger PnL | A YES+NO lock is marked at mid on both legs, so the crossed spread plus fees appear as an unrealized loss until settlement. `expected_locked_pnl_if_settlement_equivalent` is the payout guaranteed *if* both legs settle on the same event; it is positive whenever a trade was made. |
| The control track shows PnL | `ungated_cross_venue_macro` trades what the gate refused and is flagged `settlement_risk`. Its numbers measure what price-only logic would have done; they are not realisable because the legs may settle on different events. |
| Many `vetoed_candidates` | The matcher refuses to pair a September contract with December, `>25bps` with `25 bps`, or a hike with a cut. These never reach the gate and are listed in the report for audit. |

Evidence: `tests/test_settlement_gate.py` (30 single-field mutations each refused with the catching stage's reason; unmodified and complement pairs admitted), `tests/test_gated_track.py` (zero orders on mismatched pairs with a 20-cent gap; gate report emitted with zero candidates and zero admits), `tests/test_paper_edge.py`, the committed `dashboard/public/artifacts/gate_report_network.json` (2026-09-14: 8 candidates / 0 admitted) and `gate_report_fixtures.json` (3 candidates / 1 admitted: the Fed fixture pair, which is the reference for a fully admissible pair).

## 19. Known gaps / not validated

* No live or demo-authenticated path exists; everything past `Settings.live_enabled` is a stub by design.
* `apps/dashboard_api.py` has no authentication (unchanged from the original design; README warns).
* Cross-venue candidate generation on live data now finds Fed-decision pairs (4.5) but no CPI/GDP/unemployment pairs yet: Polymarket's macro events use point buckets and different phrasing from Kalshi's threshold ladders, and the Gamma search terms are a short hard-coded list (`venues/polymarket/client.py::DEFAULT_MACRO_SEARCH`).
* No live pair can be admitted until an operator authors fingerprints for both markets (4b); the gate's positive path is validated on fixtures and synthetic pairs only.
* `parse_fed_bucket` is regex-based and returns `None` on anything ambiguous; it decides a *reject* stage only, never admission on its own.
* Ledger aggregation across tracks sums thirteen independent `1000` starting-cash books (`portfolio.starting_cash = 13000` on the full board, `3000` on the arb-only board, `2000` on the FLB-only board, `1000` on the fade-the-tourist board, `1000` on the whale/noise board); per-track figures are under `portfolio.by_track` and the primary track under `portfolio.primary`.
* Daily-loss reset semantics (1.4) are cumulative, not calendar-based.
* The `news_underreaction` lane's signal→probability mapping, pre-signal price provenance and drift horizon are all UNKNOWN; see section 9 and `docs/NEWS_UNDERREACTION.md`.
* `tennis_basis`: the live gap distribution against the free feed is UNKNOWN (no key in the build environment), the bookmaker-vs-venue settlement basis is documented but not modelled, and retirements are only detectable from an operator results file; see section 13 and `docs/TENNIS_BASIS.md`.
* The `category_specialist` hypothesis is underpowered (0 of the pre-registered 30 resolved network follows); the lane is Polymarket-only, its category taxonomy is heuristic and its follow PnL is pre-fee; see section 9b and `docs/SPECIALIST_SCOREBOARD.md`.
* `weather_bucket_edge`: the edge is UNKNOWN until the register accumulates ≥ 30 venue-settled city-days; ensemble calibration, grid-vs-station representativeness and the METAR reconstruction of NOAA's hourly column are documented assumptions, not validations; see section 15 and `docs/WEATHER_BUCKETS.md`.
* Polymarket arb tracks: subset NegRisk conversions, implication-based combinatorial arbitrage across events, converter fee, maker/taker rebates and legging beyond the one-tick buffer are not modelled (see `docs/POLYMARKET_ARB.md`, "Known limits").
* FLB maker fill probabilities on the snapshot track (10.4) remain assumptions; the additive queue-aware sim (10.10) replaces them with an explicit L2+tape model whose live edge is still UNKNOWN; the ex-post trade tape cannot distinguish opening from closing trades, and the sample covers only the newest settled markets per series (see section 10 and `docs/FLB_RUNBOOK.md`).
* Fade-the-tourist (section 17): the classifier thresholds are unfitted rules, the replay's 2-tick spread is a guess, `late_chase` is adverse-selected on in-play tennis, and the sample is the newest 60 settled markets per series on one day; the pooled pass criterion fails and no live canary is warranted (see `docs/FADE_THE_TOURIST.md`).
* The self-logged book archive (section 11) is polled REST L2: it cannot recover queue position or FIFO priority, misses everything between polls, and its Kalshi snapshots have no venue timestamp. `SidecarSource` (official RSS / free weather) is a protocol stub with no implementation.
* Tennis whale copy (section 12): exit liquidity, wallet fragmentation across proxies, cross-sport farming and the completeness of the venue's own `takerOnly` tape are not validated; the resolved universe is the newest ~2,000 closed tennis markets above the volume floor (Gamma refuses deeper offsets).
* `weather_dead_bucket` (section 14): 0 settled positions, so the ≥ 2¢ hypothesis is UNKNOWN; on the one committed cycle every dead leg had no takeable NO ask (the queue sits at 0.999), the WRH table's tie rounding and late corrections are handled fail-closed rather than modelled, the "late-day, falling" rule is a heuristic the kill rule polices, "lowest temperature" events and non-airport resolution sources are refused, and there is no resting/maker variant; see `docs/WEATHER_DEAD_BUCKET.md`.
* `weather_naive_ensemble` / `weather_calibrated_ensemble` (section 16): A/B edge UNKNOWN until each lane reaches the pre-registered N; see `docs/WEATHER_CALIBRATION.md`.
* Weather calibration A/B (section 15, `docs/WEATHER_CALIBRATION.md`): the pre-registered settled-EV test is UNDERPOWERED (0 settled admissions; needs a daily loop); calibration lead is ~24 h longer than trading lead until live samples replace the archive; the settled bucket's midpoint stands in for the station reading; Jinan's registry coordinate is probably not its resolution station; whether the market already prices the per-city biases is the open question.
* The whale/noise track (section 18) replays a polled tape: the book used for a quote can be up to one poll cycle old, maker fills are simulated (pessimistically) rather than observed, marks over a short capture window are mostly in-play noise, and "whale" is a size class whose EV was **not** verified in the sample harvested here.
